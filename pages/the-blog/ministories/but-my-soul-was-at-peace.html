But my soul was at peace

October 26th, 2026

The day i was born, the world, or so I was told, was engulfed in black clouds, light flashing and thunder cracking at every moment. Some fearful priests said it signaled the rapture, but of course it was nothing.

At age 2 the children in my neighborhood frequently called me 'enfant du diable'. They often threw stones at me, breaking my still weak bones. I bled and bled, and cut and bled. So much blood for a young child. Their parents were somehow even worse. They gossiped that I was the antichrist. Spreading lies about me and my family. At that age, I had already decided that nothing mattered. Anything that you would do would only make things worse. So as I was still a child new from the womb, the world had given me no choice. Nothing mattered and neither did I, and I have accepted that since.

At age 10, my father fell severely ill. St. Anthony's Fire was usually a death sentence. If not now, then in a few months. If not then, you better be counting your days because that's all you get. My father died 2 months later. He led a fearless fight but it didn't matter. As nothing ever did. At the funeral i saw a peculiar shadow. Not of a child I had ever seen before. He was unusually joyful at that mournful time. His grin followed into his shadow. I felt his soul laughing as my father was buried. It was probably nothing. Maybe he knew, like me, that nothing mattered. Little did i know, that strange child would be my one and only friend for a long time. I spoke to him after the funeral. He did not say a word back as I dragged on and on about pointless topics. Stopping only to ask his name. He answered 'Anguisseus'. It was a funny name, as in it was not a name at all. But it had been given to him so I accepted it. We would talk for hours. I would learn to pronounce his name better each time. Though I never grasped the letters as perfectly as he did. He said neither his parents knew how to speak it with such proficiency as him. And I felt a little better knowing not even adults could speak so profoundly.

At age 15 I now saw the good in life again. As I once did 14 years ago. Anguisseus had shown me how to rebel. Life was perfect. I could see the little joyus moments in life. I could feel the sun osculate my skin again. A few days after my 16th birthday I met a beautiful lady. She had hair so perfect it flowed with the wind. Her face, so pale I could've mistaken her for royalty. Her name was Marion. Together with her, and Anguisseus, we were the most troublesome group of kids ever.

One day, Marion had trouble pronouncing Anguisseus' name, as was a rite of passage for everyone. He in turn gave himself the nickname 'Dieus'. The local church did not like that we called him god. But they couldn't do anything. As long as we ran away every time we did something even remotely treacherous, and we ran far. They could not punish us. This was however foolish of us, as none of us had remembered that our parents went to church every sunday. We were reprimanded and our parents secluded us from each other's company for a week afterwards. But that did not stop us.

At age 18, Me and Dieus wore Marion's dresses and she wore some of my clothes to church. We were as close as beaten to death as one can be before being actually beaten. We were young adults and the local pastor was growing old. He couldn't run after us like he could a couple years ago. A week after this event. I confessed my feelings about Marion to Dieus. He urged me to marry her. As he said she had confessed to him as well. Dieus was a trustworthy person. And he was also one of my favourite people. Even more so my mother. And Marion even more so. The next day I proposed to Marion. She surprisingly said yes. Her father was a bit disappointed in her choice, as he wanted her to marry someone of royalty as she was worthy of. But he was supportive nonetheless. My mother was proud of me, as she so blindly was every time.

In the days leading up to my marriage, Anguisseus kept on nagging me for just the two of us to go on one last adventure. "You should enjoy one last moment of freedom!" He exclaimed, slightly giggling. But that laughter seemed aware of itself. I finally caved and he proposed we go to the Fontainebleau Forest. I'd heard tales of hellish creatures hunting in the grim nights of that forest. But that's all they were, tales. We prepared for a day's worth of travel. The sun came up on that day. So dull, yet so bright. I wonder if the sun ever gets tired of repeating its monotonous life. I would assume so. We set off. I left Marion a good-bye kiss. We both blushed a little. On the long walk towards the forest we talked about how he'd helped me so much. Dieus was good for me. I'd been so depressed. Maybe I still am a little. But he showed me the beauty in life. The little things. The insects that crawled around us. The trees that made sunsets so worthwhile. The sky, so blue. If not for him, nothing would have ever mattered. Not Marion. Not my own self. Not anyone around me. I was thankful for Dieus.

As we approached the forest. The sky darkened. And by the time we were deep enough to set up our little hike for the next morning, the moon had risen. Although it didn't look like the moon we saw every night. Its colour had shifted to the yellow of someones eyes struck with jaundice. And as the night progressed, as the moon rose above us, its colour changed again. Now a deep orange. I was getting fearful. Maybe those tales had been true. I wonder if Marion was concerned for me. Anguisseus assured me it was nothing.

"It's just a trick of the light" he said. I believed him of course. We marched on forward. I was getting very anxious and a bit clumsy with my footing. I should have trusted Anguisseus and just ignored the moon. But that of course was my mistake. I urged Anguisseus to walk faster and in my haste, forgot to look below myself. I fell into a pit of mud. The more I tried to escape the more it tightened around me. Dieus tried to save me but he was not strong enough to pull the weight of me, and the bags I held, out by himself. It was not his mistake of course. It was only mine of course. In that moment I came to my old self. I forgot Marion. I forgot I had become fond of this meaningless life god gave me. I gave up. In that moment, I

accepted that I was as good as dead. I, in a sense, was dead of course. I could see the fear on his face. He couldn't see me die right in front of his eyes. How would he face Marion. How would he face my mother. I told him it was okay. He couldn't have done anything. He shouldn't have a guilty conscience. He shold've been lucky he survived.

"NO!" He yelled out. Or did he...? Did he yell? Or did he softly cry. That sound. It was... Unlike anything I'd ever heard. I felt so... No. There are no words. There exist no words in my language nor even this very lexicon that can describe what i felt as he said what he said. I felt so powerful, so powerless. Such happiness, I cried. Such sadness, I sobbed. It was pure ecstasy, was it not? That moment of joy, of laughter, of fear, of anger, of life, of death. That moment. That little slice of time.

I was...?

I'm standing near a tree.

How did I...? I...?

IT WAS ANGUISSEUS OF COURSE! He pulled me out! That MADMAN!

Why can't I move...?

Where am I really?

The moon. That blood red... Oh.

Anguisseus dressed me in old ancient clothes. Clothes so distinctly rugged and worn. They had torn from edge to edge. They had been worn for ages now. Before I was ever born. Perhaps even before the sun had begun to burn. I could tell their age just by them touching my skin. They were so... I was so peaceful. I could still feel, it was primitive however. My emotions were still in a flux after all. I was feeling everything yet nothing. My body had given up. But my soul was at peace.

Anguisseus placed a crown of thorns upon my head. OH! The agony! OH! My body squirmed. And it squirmed. Moving erratically. And my head was cut and cut and it bled. And it bled and bled. But my soul was at peace. What did the others feel in this moment? They were lesser. They felt every cut.

Every twist of their own self, their very essence burning.

Anguisseus loved me did he not?

Dieus...? Oh.

He did. He loved me. That's why my soul was at peace. That's why i was in a state of pure euphoria. I was in that moment ascended. I was so exalted. I'm surprised he didn't do it sooner. My body was being torn. Each swipe of his heavenly claw gripped deeper into my organs. My body tried to scream. But he had already cut out my throat. But my soul was at peace.

Anguisseus why not show me your godly face before? I asked. His face already deep into my flesh, covered in blood. His eyes glaring deep through my soul. But he didn't have eyes did he? They were merely projections. His head adorning horns so beautiful. They were truly brilliant. Twisting and splitting, turning and breaking, yet held together. I didn't feel a thing of course. For though my body was alive, my soul was at peace.
