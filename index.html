The Children of Nehras

7th August, 2025

Fifty children are thrust into a grotesque imitation of nature, a sealed room where nothing organic survives. The walls are wrapped in warped wallpaper mimicking an open field, the ground is covered in abrasive plastic turf that cuts into their ankles, and a vast screen above plays an endless loop of a prerecorded sky. Stripped of answers, identity, and escape, they're left only with each other and the disembodied voice always watching, from the walls.

Who is Ornithos?

Ornithos

Who am I?

A psychological fiction writer trying to make a name in the literary world. My novels are usually short, and have themes of trauma, existential crisis, and over all disturbance.

Besides my books, I also come up with ideas that can't be published into a fully fleshed story with character arcs, etc. That's why I've created a blog for these "mini" stories that you can enjoy while I'm busy on a different project. Other than my literary work, I am also an artist and you can find some of my work on Instagram.
